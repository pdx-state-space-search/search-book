## Wikipedia adaptation schema for future revisions

For each actual adaptation, record:

- Article title and language.
- Exact permanent revision URL with oldid.
- Article URL and contributor-history link.
- Retrieval date and the applicable license/version.
- Specific source section(s) or figure/file page.
- Specific destination chapter/heading.
- What was reused and how it was edited, abridged, corrected or
  translated; retain earlier change notices as applicable.
- Exact reader-facing credit and where it appears.
- Any additional source notices or file-specific license terms.

Do not populate this ledger with invented revision IDs or with
articles that were not actually adapted. If a later editor reuses
Wikipedia material, update src/attribution.md and the affected
section before building/distributing the revision.
