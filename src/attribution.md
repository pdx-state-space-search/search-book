## Wikipedia provenance

No Wikipedia article passages, tables, pseudocode listings,
or figures have been incorporated into this draft. The
previous Wikipedia outline influenced topic selection;
chapter explanations and examples were written for this
book. There are therefore no adapted article revisions to
list for this version.

Future Wikipedia adaptations must carry a specific credit
near the affected section and an entry in this page and the
source ledger. Each entry must identify the article,
permanent revision URL, contributor-history link, applicable
license, book section, and changes made. A link to an article
without a record of what was reused is not the detailed
attribution intended for this project. A Wikimedia image
would additionally require its own file-page credit and
license check.

The editorial policy follows the
[Wikimedia Terms of Use, section 7](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Terms_of_Use#7._Licensing_of_Content)
and the CC BY-SA 4.0 license linked above.
