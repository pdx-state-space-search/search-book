# Bibliography

This list collects sources cited in the text and the weekly
reading recommendations. Citations in the chapters identify
sources for particular concepts and claims; the optional
"Further reading" sections suggest how to explore selected
works. Inclusion here does not make a work required reading.

## Bonet and Geffner (2001) {#bonet-geffner-2001}

Blai Bonet and Héctor Geffner.
[“Planning as Heuristic Search.”](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0004-3702(01)00108-4)
*Artificial Intelligence* 129(1–2), 5–33, 2001.

## Crawford and Auton (1993) {#crawford-auton-1993}

James M. Crawford and Larry D. Auton.
[“Experimental Results on the Crossover Point in
Satisfiability Problems.”](https://cdn.aaai.org/AAAI/1993/AAAI93-004.pdf)
*Proceedings of AAAI-93*, 21–27, 1993.

## Davis, Logemann, and Loveland (1962) {#davis-logemann-loveland-1962}

Martin Davis, George Logemann, and Donald Loveland.
[“A Machine Program for Theorem-Proving.”](https://doi.org/10.1145/368273.368557)
*Communications of the ACM* 5(7), 394–397, 1962.

## Dechter and Pearl (1985) {#dechter-pearl-1985}

Rina Dechter and Judea Pearl.
[“Generalized Best-First Search Strategies and the
Optimality of A*.”](https://doi.org/10.1145/3828.3830)
*Journal of the ACM* 32(3), 505–536, 1985.

## Dijkstra (1959) {#dijkstra-1959}

Edsger W. Dijkstra.
[“A Note on Two Problems in Connexion with Graphs.”](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01386390)
*Numerische Mathematik* 1, 269–271, 1959.

## Glover (1989) {#glover-1989}

Fred Glover.
[“Tabu Search—Part I.”](https://doi.org/10.1287/ijoc.1.3.190)
*ORSA Journal on Computing* 1(3), 190–206, 1989.

## Hansen and Zhou (2007) {#hansen-zhou-2007}

Eric A. Hansen and Rong Zhou.
[“Anytime Heuristic Search.”](https://doi.org/10.1613/jair.2096)
*Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research* 28, 267–297, 2007.

## Hart, Nilsson, and Raphael (1968) {#hart-nilsson-raphael-1968}

Peter E. Hart, Nils J. Nilsson, and Bertram Raphael.
[“A Formal Basis for the Heuristic Determination of
Minimum Cost Paths.”](https://doi.org/10.1109/TSSC.1968.300136)
*IEEE Transactions on Systems Science and Cybernetics*
4(2), 100–107, 1968.

## Johnson et al. (1989) {#johnson-et-al-1989}

David S. Johnson, Cecilia R. Aragon, Lyle A. McGeoch,
and Catherine Schevon.
[“Optimization by Simulated Annealing: An Experimental
Evaluation; Part I, Graph Partitioning.”](https://doi.org/10.1287/opre.37.6.865)
*Operations Research* 37(6), 865–892, 1989.

## Junttila (2023) {#junttila-2023}

Tomi Junttila.
[“CNF Translations.”](https://users.aalto.fi/~tjunttil/2023-DP-AUT/notes-sat/cnf2.html)
Course notes for *Propositional Satisfiability and SAT Solvers*,
Aalto University, 2023. In particular, “Method 3: The
Tseitin-translation” gives a linear-size construction with
auxiliary variables and its assignment correspondence.

## Karp (1972) {#karp-1972}

Richard M. Karp.
[“Reducibility Among Combinatorial Problems.”](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4684-2001-2_9)
In *Complexity of Computer Computations*, 85–103, 1972.

## Khemani (2024) {#khemani-2024}

Deepak Khemani. *Search Methods in Artificial Intelligence*.
Cambridge University Press, 2024.
[Publisher contents.](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/search-methods-in-artificial-intelligence/contents/D2DE03267943183B66EF635E65048691)
The contents informed topic coverage; this is not a link
to an openly available full text.

## Kirkpatrick, Gelatt, and Vecchi (1983) {#kirkpatrick-gelatt-vecchi-1983}

S. Kirkpatrick, C. D. Gelatt Jr., and M. P. Vecchi.
[“Optimization by Simulated Annealing.”](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.220.4598.671)
*Science* 220(4598), 671–680, 1983.

## Kishimoto, Fukunaga, and Botea (2013) {#kishimoto-fukunaga-botea-2013}

Akihiro Kishimoto, Alex Fukunaga, and Adi Botea.
[“Evaluation of a Simple, Scalable, Parallel Best-First
Search Strategy.”](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.artint.2012.10.007)
*Artificial Intelligence* 195, 222–248, 2013.
[Author preprint.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.3204)

## Knuth and Moore (1975) {#knuth-moore-1975}

Donald E. Knuth and Ronald W. Moore.
[“An Analysis of Alpha-Beta Pruning.”](https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702(75)90019-3)
*Artificial Intelligence* 6(4), 293–326, 1975.

## Kocsis and Szepesvári (2006) {#kocsis-szepesvari-2006}

Levente Kocsis and Csaba Szepesvári.
[“Bandit Based Monte-Carlo Planning.”](https://doi.org/10.1007/11871842_29)

## Korf (1985) {#korf-1985}

Richard E. Korf.

*Artificial Intelligence* 27(1), 97–109, 1985.

## Korf (1993) {#korf-1993}

Richard E. Korf.

Richard E. Korf.

*Artificial Intelligence* 106(2), 181–203, 1998.
[Instructor-hosted paper.](https://web.cecs.pdx.edu/bart/cs510cs/papers/korf-ckk.pdf)

## Korf and Felner (2002) {#korf-felner-2002}

Steven Minton, Mark D. Johnston, Andrew B. Philips,

## Mitchell (2006) {#mitchell-2006}

Melanie Mitchell.
[“Complex Systems: Network Thinking.”](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.artint.2006.10.002)

[Author's working paper, SFI 2006-10-036.](https://sfi-edu.s3.amazonaws.com/sfi-edu/production/uploads/sfi-com/dev/uploads/filer/67/c4/67c4bcce-7e58-4819-8aed-9a67163dd441/06-10-036.pdf)

## Okumura (2023a) {#okumura-2023a}

Keisuke Okumura.

## Shannon (1950) {#shannon-1950}

Claude E. Shannon.

## Sutton and Barto (2018) {#sutton-barto-2018}

Richard S. Sutton and Andrew G. Barto.
[*Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction*.](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262039246/reinforcement-learning/)

Markov property and retaining the relevant past in a state.

## Tsang (1993) {#tsang-1993}

Edward P. K. Tsang.
[*Foundations of Constraint Satisfaction*.](https://www.bracil.net/edward/FCS.html)
Academic Press, 1993. The author's page also describes a
corrected 2014 reprint; our citation is to the original edition.
